\section{Basics}
\label{sec:basics}

In this section, we review the categorical semantics of simple inductive types
by means of a worked example, and then generalise it to containers and W-types.
Finally, we introduce the category of containers.

\paragraph*{Semantics of Types}

Informally, inductive types are interpreted as initial algebras of
endofunctors on \AgdaPrimitive{Type}. For example, the natural numbers together
with their constructors
\AgdaSymbol{(}%
\AgdaDatatype{ℕ}\AgdaSpace{}%
\AgdaSymbol{,}\AgdaSpace{}%
\AgdaInductiveConstructor{[z,s]}\AgdaSymbol{)}\AgdaSpace{}%
form the initial algebra of the functor
\AgdaDatatype{Maybe}\AgdaSpace{}%
\AgdaBound{X}\AgdaSpace{}%
\AgdaSymbol{=}\AgdaSpace{}%
\AgdaRecord{1}\AgdaSpace{}%
\AgdaDatatype{+}\AgdaSpace{}%
\AgdaBound{X}%
. Formally, this means that for any other \AgdaFunction{Maybe}-algebra
\AgdaSymbol{(}%
\AgdaBound{A}\AgdaSpace{}%
\AgdaSymbol{,}\AgdaSpace{}%
\AgdaBound{α}\AgdaSymbol{)}\AgdaSpace{}%
, there exists a unique folding function
\AgdaFunction{foldℕ}\AgdaSpace{}%
\AgdaSymbol{:}\AgdaSpace{}%
\AgdaSymbol{(}\AgdaFunction{Maybe}\AgdaSpace{}%
\AgdaGeneralizable{A}\AgdaSpace{}%
\AgdaSymbol{→}\AgdaSpace{}%
\AgdaGeneralizable{A}\AgdaSymbol{)}\AgdaSpace{}%
\AgdaSymbol{→}\AgdaSpace{}%
\AgdaDatatype{ℕ}\AgdaSpace{}%
\AgdaSymbol{→}\AgdaSpace{}%
\AgdaGeneralizable{A}%
\ such that the following diagram commutes:

\[
\begin{tikzcd}[column sep=huge, row sep=large]
  \AgdaFunction{Maybe}\,\AgdaDatatype{ℕ}
    \arrow[r, "\AgdaFunction{Maybe₁}\,\AgdaSymbol{(}\AgdaFunction{foldℕ}\,
      \AgdaBound{α}\AgdaSymbol{)}"]
    \arrow[d, "\AgdaInductiveConstructor{[z,s]}"] &
  \AgdaFunction{Maybe}\,\AgdaBound{A}
    \arrow[d, "\AgdaBound{α}"] \\
  \AgdaDatatype{ℕ}
    \arrow[r, dashed, "\AgdaFunction{foldℕ}\,\AgdaBound{α}"] &
  \AgdaBound{A}
\end{tikzcd}
\]

To formalise this, it suffices to construct two functions:
\AgdaFunction{commuteℕ}, which witnesses that \AgdaFunction{foldℕ} is an
algebra homomorphism, and \AgdaFunction{uniquefoldℕ}, which establishes its
uniqueness. Together, they show that \AgdaDatatype{ℕ} is the initial
\AgdaFunction{Maybe}-algebra.
