A container encodes a polynomial functor by its interpretation or extension functor \AgdaFunction{⟦\_⟧}. The type \AgdaField{S} of \emph{shapes} indexes the monomials, and the family \AgdaField{P} of \emph{positions} gives the exponent of \AgdaBound{X} in each monomial. For example, \AgdaDatatype{Maybe} is represented by \AgdaFunction{Maybeᶜ}\;\AgdaSymbol{=}\;\AgdaDatatype{Bool}\;\AgdaOperator{\AgdaInductiveConstructor{◃}}\;\AgdaFunction{isTrue}, where \AgdaFunction{isTrue} sends \AgdaInductiveConstructor{true} to \AgdaDatatype{⊤} and \AgdaInductiveConstructor{false} to \AgdaDatatype{⊥}. This captures precisely the fact that \AgdaDatatype{Maybe} has two constructors: \AgdaInductiveConstructor{nothing}, which carries no element of \AgdaBound{X}, and \AgdaInductiveConstructor{just}, which carries exactly one. Inductive functors, such as \AgdaDatatype{List}, are also containers: \AgdaFunction{Listᶜ}\;\AgdaSymbol{=}\;\AgdaDatatype{ℕ}\;\AgdaOperator{\AgdaInductiveConstructor{◃}}\;\AgdaDatatype{Fin}.
