\section{Basics}
\label{sec:basics}

In this section, we introduce the denotational semantics of simple inductive
types through a worked example, and then generalises to containers and W-types.
We then introduce the category of containers.

\paragraph*{Semantics of Types}

Informally, the semantics of inductive types says they are exactly represented
as the intial algebras of endofunctors of \AgdaPrimitive{Type}. For example,
the natural number and its unique constructors

\AgdaDatatype{ℕ}\AgdaSpace{}%
\AgdaSymbol{,}\AgdaSpace{}%
\AgdaInductiveConstructor{[z,s]}\AgdaSymbol{)}\AgdaSpace{}%
is the inital algebra of functor
\AgdaDatatype{Maybe}\AgdaSpace{}%
\AgdaBound{X}\AgdaSpace{}%
\AgdaSymbol{=}\AgdaSpace{}%
\AgdaRecord{1}\AgdaSpace{}%
\AgdaDatatype{+}\AgdaSpace{}%
\AgdaBound{X}%
. Formally, we say given any other Maybe-algebra
\AgdaSymbol{(}%
\AgdaBound{A}\AgdaSpace{}%
\AgdaSymbol{,}\AgdaSpace{}%
\AgdaBound{α}\AgdaSymbol{)}\AgdaSpace{}%
, there is an unique folding function
\AgdaFunction{foldℕ}\AgdaSpace{}%
\AgdaSymbol{:}\AgdaSpace{}%
\AgdaSymbol{(}\AgdaFunction{Maybe}\AgdaSpace{}%
\AgdaGeneralizable{A}\AgdaSpace{}%
\AgdaSymbol{→}\AgdaSpace{}%

\AgdaDatatype{ℕ}\AgdaSpace{}%
\AgdaSymbol{→}\AgdaSpace{}%
\AgdaGeneralizable{A}%
, such makes the following diagram commutes:

\[
\begin{tikzcd}[column sep=huge, row sep=large]
  \AgdaFunction{Maybe}\,\AgdaDatatype{ℕ}
    \arrow[r, "\AgdaFunction{Maybe₁}\,\AgdaSymbol{(}\AgdaFunction{foldℕ}\,
      \AgdaBound{α}\AgdaSymbol{)}"]
    \arrow[d, "\AgdaInductiveConstructor{[z,s]}"] &
  \AgdaFunction{Maybe}\,\AgdaBound{A}
    \arrow[d, "\AgdaBound{α}"] \\
  \AgdaDatatype{ℕ}
    \arrow[r, dashed, "\AgdaFunction{foldℕ}\,\AgdaBound{α}"] &
  \AgdaBound{A}
\end{tikzcd}

To formalise this explicitly, it suffices to construct two functions:
\AgdaFunction{commuteℕ}, which witnesses that \AgdaFunction{foldℕ} is a
homomorphism, and \AgdaFunction{uniquefoldℕ}, which proves the
uniqueness condition. Together, these establish that \AgdaDatatype{ℕ} is the
initial algebra.
