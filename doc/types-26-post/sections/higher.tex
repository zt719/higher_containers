\section{Fixed points in cartesian closed bicategories}

Taking a step back from the specific notions of this paper, we can consider abstractly the question of existence of fixed points, and obtain a no-go theorem for the naïve setup where the kind ∗ is interpreted as \AgdaPrimitive{Type} and function types are preserved by the semantics, so that for instance the interpretation of a container, $C$ of kind $(∗ → ∗) → ∗$ becomes an operation \AgdaSymbol{(}\AgdaPrimitive{Type}\;\AgdaSymbol{→}\;\AgdaPrimitive{Type}\AgdaSymbol{)}\;\AgdaSymbol{→}\;\AgdaPrimitive{Type}. Our chosen setting here is the notion of a cartesian closed bicategory, and the naïve semantics would be a cartesian closed pseudo-functor into the kinds-as-types bicategory described below.

\begin{definition}
Let $f : A×B → B$ be a 1-cell in a cartesian closed bicategory $ℂ$. An $A$-fixed point of $f$ is a 1-cell $x : A → B$ such that $f ∘ 〈id , x 〉≅ x$.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}
Fixed points are preserved by cartesian closed pseudofunctors (up to an isomorphism).
\end{lemma}

\begin{definition}
An internal fixed point operator in a cartesian closed bicategory $ℂ$, is a 1-cell $y : [A,A] → A$, such that $y ≅ id · y (= ev 〈 id  , y 〉)$.
\end{definition}

\begin{verbatim}
          y
    [A,A] ──> A
     │       ^
id,y │  ≅   ╱
     v     ╱  ev
    [A,A]×A
\end{verbatim}

\begin{lemma}
Given an internal fixed point operator $y : [B,B] → B$, and a 1-cell $f : A×B → B$, we obtain an $A$-fixed point $y ∘ Λ f : A → B$ of $f$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{lemma}
An internal fixed point operator is exactly an $[A,A]$-fixed point of $ev : [A,A]×A → A$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proposition}
A product preserving pseudofuncto preserves internal fixed point operators (up to an isomorphism).
\end{proposition}

\begin{definition}
In any cartesian closed bicategory $ℂ$ and any 0-cell $A$ in it, let $Φ_A$ be the map $Φ_A : [[A,A],A] → [[A,A],A]$, defined by $Φ_A = Λ(π₂ · (π₁ · π₂))$ (which is $λ M f. f (M f)$, via the Curry--Howard correspondence).
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}
Any fixed point operator $y : [A,A] → A$ gives rise to a $1$-fixed point $⌜y⌝ : 1 → [[A,A],A]$ of $Φ_A$. Conversely, every $1$-fixed point $r : 1 → [[A,A],A]$ of $Φ_A$ gives rise to a fixed point operator $⌞r⌟ : [A,A] → A$.
\end{lemma}

\textbf{Remark:} Since λ-terms are essentially preserved by cartesian closed pseudo-functors, so is $Φ_A$ (up to a relating isomorphism).

\begin{proposition}
A cartesian closed bicategory with fixed points for all maps $[[A,A],A] → [[A,A],A]$, has a fixed point operator $y : [A,A] → A$ I.e. an external fixed point operation of rank $n+2$ gives rise to an internal fixed point operation of rank $n$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{definition}
Let $\mathcal{T}$ be the bicategory with 0-cells being lists of kinds (representing products), 1-cells $\mathcal{T} {\vec k}␣{\vec l} ≔ \AgdaPrimitive{Type}{\vec k} → \AgdaPrimitive{Type}{\vec l}$ and 2-cells $\mathcal{T}{\vec k}␣{\vec l}␣f␣g = ∏_{x: \AgdaPrimitive{Type}{\vec k}}\big(f␣x → g␣x\big)$.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}
$\mathcal{T}$ has no internal fixed point operator for $[[∗,∗],∗]$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{theorem}
No cartesian closed bicategory ℂ with a cartesian closed pseudofunctor $F : ℂ → \mathcal{T}$ can have a fixed point for $Φ_A$ for any $A$ such that $F␣A ≅ ∗$.
\end{theorem}
