\section{Metatheory}
\label{sec:metatheory}

Our metatheory is \emph{HoTT}. Our development is formalisd in Cubical Agda.

The categories studied in this paper are wild categories. Each consists of a
class of objects and a \emph{class} of morphisms, equipped with identity
morphisms and a composition operations that satisfy the left and right unit laws
and associativity. That is, we do not require morphisms to be \AgdaFunction{hSet}
