\section{Second-Order Containers}
\label{sec:second}

The example of Section~\ref{sec:bush} generalizes to arbitrary (potentially infinitary) second-order polynomial functors on types. By a second-order polynomial we mean a polynomial in both a variable $x$ and a function variable $f$. For instance, the signature of \AgdaDatatype{Bush} corresponds to $h (f , x) = 1 + x * f (f (x))$.

We introduce the following syntax for second-order containers:

The syntax is analogous to the first-order case: \AgdaField{S} indexes the monomials, while the families \AgdaField{PX} and \AgdaField{PF} give the exponents of the variable $x$ and of the function variable $f$, respectively. The interesting component is \AgdaField{RF}, which recursively describes the inner polynomial to which each occurrence of $f$ is applied. For example, the signature of \AgdaDatatype{Bush} is represented by taking \AgdaField{S} to be \AgdaDatatype{Bool}. At shape \AgdaInductiveConstructor{false}, which stands for the summand $1$, both \AgdaField{PX} and \AgdaField{PF} are \AgdaDatatype{⊥}. At shape \AgdaInductiveConstructor{true}, which stands for the summand $x * f (f (x))$, both are \AgdaDatatype{⊤}, and for the single outer occurrence of $f$, \AgdaField{RF} gives the \AgdaRecord{2Cont} representing the inner polynomial $h' (f , x) = f (x)$. The explicit type interpretation is given by \AgdaFunction{2⟦\_⟧→ᶜ}.

Second-order containers also form a category, whose morphisms are given by:

The components \AgdaField{fS} and \AgdaField{fPX} are as in the first-order case: shapes are mapped forwards, and the positions of $x$ backwards. Likewise, \AgdaField{fPF} maps the positions of $f$ backwards, choosing for each occurrence of $f$ in the target the occurrence in the source it comes from. Finally, \AgdaField{fRF} recursively gives a morphism between the inner polynomials of these two occurrences. Just as a container morphism encodes a polymorphic function, a second-order container morphism encodes a second-order polymorphic function. For instance, Haskell lift function for maybe monad transformer \texttt{lift :: Monad m => m a -> MaybeT m a} can be seen as a polymophic function from \AgdaFunction{IdentityT} to \AgdaFunction{MaybeT}. We omit the definition of interpretation here.

\paragraph*{Second-Order W-type}

Does every second-order container have an initial algebra? We answer this question positively by constructing second-order W-types. We first construct \AgdaFunction{2W}, which must be a fixed-point of \AgdaFunction{app}\;\AgdaBound{H}:

\[ \AgdaFunction{2W}\;\AgdaBound{H}\;\AgdaFunction{≃}\;\AgdaFunction{app}\;\AgdaBound{H}\;(\AgdaFunction{2W}\;\AgdaBound{H}) \]

Splitting this explicitly into shapes and positions yields:

\[ \AgdaDatatype{2WS}\;\AgdaBound{H}\;\AgdaOperator{\AgdaInductiveConstructor{◃}}\;\AgdaDatatype{2WP}\;\AgdaBound{H}\;\AgdaFunction{≃}\;\AgdaFunction{appS}\;\AgdaBound{H}\;(\AgdaFunction{2W}\;\AgdaBound{H})\;\AgdaOperator{\AgdaInductiveConstructor{◃}}\;\AgdaFunction{appP}\;\AgdaBound{H}\;(\AgdaFunction{2W}\;\AgdaBound{H}) \]

However, Agda rejects both \AgdaDatatype{2WS} and \AgdaDatatype{2WP} as not strictly positive, even though they are morally strictly positive. This is because \AgdaFunction{appS} and \AgdaFunction{appP} are defined by recursion, and Agda cannot unfold such recursive definitions when checking strict positivity. Our solution is to generalize the original equation to:

\[
\AgdaFunction{2W-d}\;\AgdaBound{H'}\;\AgdaBound{H}\;\AgdaFunction{≃}\;\AgdaFunction{app}\;\AgdaBound{H'}\;(\AgdaFunction{2W-d}\;\AgdaBound{H'}\;\AgdaBound{H})
\]

Splitting again into shapes and positions:

\[
\AgdaRecord{2WS-d}\;\AgdaBound{H'}\;\AgdaBound{H}\;\AgdaOperator{\AgdaInductiveConstructor{◃}}\;\AgdaDatatype{2WP-d}\;\AgdaBound{H'}\;\AgdaBound{H}\;\AgdaFunction{≃}\;\AgdaFunction{appS}\;\AgdaBound{H'}\;(\AgdaFunction{2W-d}\;\AgdaBound{H'}\;\AgdaBound{H})\;\AgdaOperator{\AgdaInductiveConstructor{◃}}\;\AgdaFunction{appP}\;\AgdaBound{H'}\;(\AgdaFunction{2W-d}\;\AgdaBound{H'}\;\AgdaBound{H})
