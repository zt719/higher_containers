\section{Settings}
\label{sec:settings}

Our metatheory is HoTT, and we formalise this work in Cubical Agda. Equality is
given by the path type
\AgdaPrimitive{Path}\AgdaSpace{}%
\AgdaBound{x}\AgdaSpace{}%
\AgdaBound{y}%
, or
\AgdaBound{x}\AgdaSpace{}%
\AgdaFunction{≡}\AgdaSpace{}%
\AgdaBound{y}%
, which is provided primitively in the cubical setting: a path is a function
from an abstract interval whose endpoints are \AgdaBound{x} and \AgdaBound{y}.
One benefit of using extensional type theory is that, function extensionality
\AgdaFunction{funExt} is derivable and computes: two functions are equal if they
agree on every input, rather than being definitionally equal. Cubical Agda also
supports heterogeneous equality primitively through \AgdaPrimitive{PathP}.

We do not assume axiom K (uniqueness of identity proofs), and reason about
equality only through the J-rule (path induction). We write
\AgdaPrimitive{Type} for the universe of types, whose elements are \emph{not}
assumed to be homotopy sets (\AgdaFunction{hSet}). At the same time, we do not
rely on any higher path structure of the types we study: our constructions
neither assume truncation nor make use of higher paths. Accordingly, the
categories considered in this paper are \emph{wild categories}: the morphisms
between two objects form an arbitrary \AgdaPrimitive{Type} rather than an
\AgdaFunction{hSet}, the unit and associativity laws hold up to paths, and no
higher coherence conditions between these laws are required.
