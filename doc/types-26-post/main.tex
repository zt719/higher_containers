\begin{abstract}
Containers represent strictly positive functors on types, and every container
has an initial algebra (a W-type) and a terminal coalgebra (an M-type). Nested
datatypes such as \emph{Bush}, however, arise as fixed-points of functors on
\emph{containers} rather than on types. We introduce second-order containers,
which represent such functors, and construct their initial algebras and
terminal coalgebras (2W and 2M) using induction-induction and
coinduction-induction. We then generalize to containers at all higher kinds,
given as normal forms of a simply-typed $\lambda$-calculus representing
functions on types using infinite products and coproducts. We show that, in this setting, fixed-points
beyond the second order do not exist: a third-order fixed-point operator would
yield an internal fixed-point for every type operator, which contradicts Cantor's
theorem. We discuss ways to overcome this limitation and to further extend
higher containers. All results are formalized in Cubical Agda.
\end{abstract}
