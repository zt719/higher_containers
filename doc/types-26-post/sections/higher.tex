\section{Higher-Order Containers}
\label{sec:higher}

Our investigation does not stop at second order: many datatypes of interest have even higher kinds. Examples include monad transformers with kind \AgdaSymbol{(}\AgdaPrimitive{Type}\;\AgdaSymbol{→}\;\AgdaPrimitive{Type}\AgdaSymbol{)}\;\AgdaSymbol{→}\;\AgdaPrimitive{Type}\;\AgdaSymbol{→}\;\AgdaPrimitive{Type}, the free monad generated by a container with kind \AgdaRecord{Cont}\;\AgdaSymbol{→}\;\AgdaPrimitive{Type}\;\AgdaSymbol{→}\;\AgdaPrimitive{Type}, and even the W-type itself with kind \AgdaRecord{Cont}\;\AgdaSymbol{→}\;\AgdaPrimitive{Type}. In this section, we introduce the syntax and semantics of higher-order containers. We turn to the question of their fixed-points in Section~\ref{sec:nogo}.
